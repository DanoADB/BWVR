# Player invitations to shared music

Point at the **body of the chime** and briefly tap/release the right front trigger to invite a nearby villager to make music. Hold for at least 0.32 seconds to use normal pickup instead. On desktop, F invites and E carries. The two chime bars retain their separate note input and four-note creature-echo demonstration.

The gesture attracts one nearby willing idle villager who can see the instrument within 24 game units. Existing injury, fear, household, resource-work, social, exploration, worship, invitation-cooldown and route checks remain authoritative. A musician with low interest can decline. No villager is teleported or made to abandon a task. Candidates are considered nearest first from the existing cached population, only on a gesture.

The host walks to a reachable spot beside the instrument, even where it has no familiar gathering-place memory, gestures, and plays the existing opening notes. The instrument stays where the player arranged it. Familiar places retain their existing shelter checks and memory. The creature still chooses whether to accept using its normal utility and action coordinator. A busy creature can prevent a new offer, and an unanswered invitation times out through the existing social routine.

## Consequences and cleanup

An accepted gesture creates no played game, learned phrase, practice, resource, energy, faith or training receipt. Only an actually completed duet can create its existing performance and learning outcomes. A transient eight-second successful-gesture cooldown and exclusive existing host reservation prevent repeated input from stacking hosts.

Holding/picking up the chime, moving it away, carrying the host, pause, interruption, timeout and suspension use existing ownership/cleanup. Tracking loss, pointer movement off the body, menu/tool/zoom ownership and a changed season cancel a pending tap. A pending gesture does not save or replay. Existing performance history retains its saved format; there is no new save schema, AI executor, art mesh, physics actor, light or frame-level population scan.
