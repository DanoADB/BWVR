# Held world actions, gifts and religion

The nine-tool palette separates intentions from direct intervention. Help, Protect and Investigate place temporary floating cues that bias the creature. Rain, Wind and Plant never place signs: select, release the selection press, then aim and hold the right front trigger (desktop F). Release stops the action. Pause, tracking loss, input conflicts and dismissal also stop it. Existing pickup, right-grip zoom and feedback controls retain their bindings.

Rain uses the local twelve-unit wetting/cooling/crop-growth footprint, not a global cistern grant. It needs supported dry timber, fire or growing crops. Wind nudges supported free balls/loose blocks with ownership and speed gates. Plant uses eligible plots and real seed/timber costs, painting at most twice per simulation second. Unlimited spiritual energy remains enabled for testing; materials are still finite.

## Food and Wood

Aim at safe dry ground near villagers (30 game units) or stores (15 units). Hold to give up to three food or two timber per bundle, at most once every two simulation seconds. Food uses the existing wheat/food stock. Gifts become usable village supply immediately; a short sack/bundle display shows the delivery at the pointer. These displays are presentation, not a new pickup or gathering job. Existing food-work observation mode is preserved.

Accept only actual unreserved storage room, with proportional amounts near capacity. Full storage rejects without spending, evidence or faith. Normal tuning costs six spiritual energy per accepted delivery; unlimited testing bypasses this. Gift totals and the shared cooldown persist, so reload cannot replay a held receipt.

## Witnessed religion

Each villager has saved faith in [0,1], independently of trust/affinity. Only active, unheld villagers within 40 game units, facing the delivery with a clear line of sight, receive credit. An accepted witnessed full bundle raises faith by 0.04, proportional for a partial bundle. Each witness has a five-second cooldown and a bounded saved receipt count. Nearby villagers looking away or behind an obstruction gain nothing. An appreciative gesture and existing heart icon communicate the response without a new permanent HUD. Village faith is the mean of current villagers' individual faith values.

Provision evidence is attributed to the player. It neither creates a creature completion/teaching lesson nor counts as a rain/healing miracle. No new village jobs, global morality, guaranteed creature orders or worship economy are introduced.

## Validation

Editor model/runtime checks cover stock, storage limits, cooldowns, save compatibility, finite inputs, witness range/facing/occlusion, held input/release, paused/suspended actions, local rain and player provenance. Final results and presentation checks are recorded in GOD_TOOL_ART.md after the live run. Quest comfort/readability and device cost require a headset playtest.
