# Music stays at a familiar gathering place

S4 increment, 2026-10-27. Placing the existing chime at a familiar gathering site can now establish a music spot. A willing villager approaches it, signals and plays the existing short invitation without fetching it away to the creature. The creature remains free to choose another action.

## Player interaction and limits

Move and release the chime within 18 terrain units of an existing non-bridge gathering site. The prospective performer must already have a recorded visit to that site. Their normal social selection, invitation cooldown, trust, injury, work/carry ownership and creature-availability gates still apply. This does not guarantee a performance on placement or give a new command button.

For an eligible in-place offer, the villager looks for a body-clear, reachable standing position approximately three terrain units from the instrument, on the side away from the creature. Up to five nearby directions are considered. A paused/locked site or unavailable position declines the offer rather than carrying away the arranged instrument. An unfamiliar or distant chime retains its existing portable gift/offer behavior.

The existing social motor owns the villager's approach and invitation. The instrument's host reservation prevents another performer from taking it. The existing creature utility model and curiosity motor choose and execute the reciprocal eight-beat game. Audience registration remains bounded at three and uses real visibility. No new simulation controller or permanent object reservation is introduced.

Player pickup, a moved instrument (over half a terrain unit), site lock/pause or ordinary interruption cancels the live in-place offer. Cancellation leaves a player-held instrument held and never snaps a moved instrument back. An unanswered offer uses the existing 18-second invitation timeout and cooldown. Neither waiting nor an incomplete performance records successful music or a place visit. An active duet also cancels safely when its instrument is picked up.

## Outcomes and persistence

A completed in-place duet now records the performer's actual place experience with the existing 90-second deduplication and weather/cover rules. Shared-game, creature learning and audience outcomes keep their existing completion receipts; there is no new player contribution credit or reward multiplier. The Book already reads these place and performer memories.

Audience comfort now checks every qualifying roof near a listener through the shared shelter query, instead of stopping at the yard's first roof. Performers and listeners retain individual comfort outcomes. Existing role/work priorities, food observation and water development focus are unchanged.

No new saved fields. Confirmed music/place memories persist through the existing save system. Active offers remain transient and are not replayed on reload. A known place is established from previous instrumented visits, not reconstructed from old geometry.
