# Regrow trees — restore harvested woodland

God Tools → **Life → Regrow trees** equips a held growth brush. Aim at one of the village's harvested tree stumps, or its growing sapling, and hold the right front trigger (desktop F). Green candidate rings identify depleted managed sites. A green pointer means the current site is usable; green motes and the growing tree show the effect. Release to stop; Empty Hand, another category/tool, menus, tracking loss and pause stop the channel.

This first forestry miracle restores **existing managed tree sites**, not arbitrary new forests. It does not resurrect fire-destroyed trees, teleport uprooted trees or replace healthy trees. It uses the existing Quaternius/environment tree art and resource regrowth visuals. Original tree placements now retain their actual model key so pine and broadleaf saplings match their mature species. The circular disk has a distinct pine silhouette rendered from the existing CC0 Quaternius Pine_1 model; its source hashes, license location and rebuild menu are in Resources/GodToolIcons/REGROW-SOURCE.txt. No new downloaded art, camera rig, controller binding or permanent HUD is introduced.

## Simulation contract

- A depleted tree must have no remaining yield or uncollected cargo. Villager leases, creature reservations, held/moved/flying/burning/destroyed trees, nearby actors and pending tree navigation updates prevent a pulse.
- The first successful pulse plants through `ResourceState.Plant`; later pulses use `ResourceState.Grow`. Each pulse contributes 20 seconds of the existing 300-second growth cycle and costs three spiritual energy. Pulses are at least 0.5 simulation seconds apart, with an eight-second/16-pulse held-stroke bound. Energy exhaustion requires a release before restarting. Unlimited development energy remains enabled.
- Saplings retain the existing root/navigation footprint and become one finite mature tree with its ordinary yield. The tool does not put timber or resin in storage, invent seed supplies, create villager work credit or guarantee creature actions. Resin renewal resumes under its existing living-tree rules.
- Preview is read-only. Successful pulses record player-miracle evidence, invalidate unrelated creature training and charge once. Unsupported aim or failed preconditions cannot spend energy.
- The ordinary source record saves planting count, growth, maturity and remaining yield. Old saves need no new schema. A reload keeps the growth but stops channel presentation; a held input must be released before starting in the restored season.
- Up to 16 existing sites have private line guides, refreshed at 4 Hz while equipped. The targeted precondition check is bounded to existing local actors/sources. A single 24-particle system supplies growth motes, with no added physical bodies, dynamic lights or particle collisions.
