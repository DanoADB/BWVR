# BWVR original procedural sound sketches

All 19 WAV files in this folder are original synthetic sounds generated for BWVR using `tools/prepare-audio.py`: seeded noise, oscillators and envelopes, with no third-party recordings or sampled music. Source files are mono PCM16, 22050 Hz. The generator and analysis report describe the process and measured levels.

The audio files are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (SPDX: CC0-1.0). No attribution is required. These are first-pass sound-design sketches, not recordings of real waves, animals or voices. No Black & White score or recordings are used. The generator follows the repository's source-code license.
